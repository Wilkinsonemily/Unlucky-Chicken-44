# 🐔 Unlucky ★ Chicken 44

**Unlucky ★ Chicken 44** is a casual PC game that combines **point-and-click searching mechanics** with **top-down RPG exploration** and **resource management**.  
After a massive thunderstorm, all **44 chickens** escape from the farm — and as the new rookie, it’s your job to bring them all back.

But beware… these chickens really don’t like the farm.

## 📖 Story

The player starts with **zero resources** — no seeds, feed, or coins.  
By exploring the world, players collect seeds, capture chickens, and return them to the farm in exchange for coins.

As the game progresses:
- Some chickens refuse seeds and require feed
- Chickens may wander into other farmers’ farms
- Nighttime rapidly drains the player’s energy

The game ends when all **44 chickens** are successfully returned to the farm.

---

## 🕹 Core Mechanics

### 🐓 Chicken Capturing
Chickens can be captured using:
- **Seeds** — collected during exploration
- **Feed** — purchased from the shop for picky chickens
- **Coins** — used to reclaim chickens from other farmers’ farms

---

### 🌱 Resource Management
- **Seeds:** Used to capture standard chickens
- **Feed:** Required for chickens that refuse seeds
- **Coins:** Earned by returning chickens and spent on advanced actions

---

### 🌙 Day–Night Cycle & Energy
- Capturing chickens consumes energy
- Energy drains continuously during nighttime
- Players must return home to sleep before collapsing
- Fainting results in a loss of coins

---

### ⚠ Game Over Conditions
Game over occurs when the player runs out of:
- Seeds
- Feed
- Coins  
*(all at the same time)*

---

### 🏆 Scoring System
- Score is determined by the number of days passed
- Fewer days result in higher scores

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Return all **44 chickens** to the main farm
- Manage resources efficiently
- Complete the game in as few days as possible
